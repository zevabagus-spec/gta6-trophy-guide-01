    Roadmap to the platinum

    Six stages, in the order to play them. Built from the official trophy descriptions: story chapters and missions first, then stranger strands, then activities, collectibles, crime and money, and the 100% cleanup last. One playthrough is enough (PowerPyx); there is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission.

      Cheats off for the whole run: PowerPyx reports turning on a cheat disables trophies (and saving). Difficulty does not matter — there are no difficulty settings and no difficulty trophies.

      Missable trophies

      PowerPyx and GTABase both flag exactly one potentially missable trophy. Every other trophy is earned by playing the story, finishing a strand, or grinding a repeatable activity — nothing else in the official descriptions ties a trophy to a one-time moment.

      Why nothing else is flagged

      • Story chapters and missions (Chapters 1–4, the final mission, Gone Fishing, Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child’s Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory): automatic on completion, unmissable by definition.
      • Stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, plus 3 more): some optional missions disappear as the story advances, but PowerPyx counts 200–300+ optional missions, far more than the 8 strands need.
      • Activities, collectibles and crime (250 stunts, 100 WAiNK spots, card sets, vaults, trucks): all repeatable in free-roam after the story, and nothing toward 100% completion is missable.
      • Choices (Actions Speak Louder): the Criminal Profile builds over 500 reported actions and can be worked in either direction; only the permanent “Irredeemable” profile is a trap, and it changes police behaviour, not trophy access.
